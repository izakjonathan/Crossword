# New-game recovery, v2.23.0

The live v2.22 catalog served all its compatible stock. At diagnosis the shared
registry contained 2,215 used answers, and the health route reported zero ready
puzzles in Easy, Medium and Hard. The 727 unused approved entries included only
29 five-letter answers and 52 three-letter answers. Existing background jobs
were exhausting or timing out while trying to fit the remaining words.

The v2.23 release adds 671 approved answers: 479 everyday words and phrases, 157
short reserve entries, and 35 entertainment answers tied to sources for works
from 2010 onward. The release preserves the pop-culture mix, capital and sports
limits, difficulty profiles, geometry, old saves, lobbies and permanent answer
history. The reviewed data records assistant editorial assessment; it is not
independent human test-solving.

The new catalog ships eight validated ready puzzles per mode. Its initial six
puzzles are mutually answer-disjoint and all ready puzzles exclude the captured
live history. A third Hard candidate was validated against the four-game mixed
replay. Compatible stock is rechecked against live history at selection and
reservation, so the count can fall when an overlapping packet is played.

Refill checks for history changes between search attempts and prioritises an
empty requested mode for one attempt. Crossword search now rejects duplicate
clues and clues revealing another answer before accepting a fill; independent
publication still rechecks those constraints. Refill remains off-thread and
bounded. A finite bank cannot promise unlimited globally unique games.

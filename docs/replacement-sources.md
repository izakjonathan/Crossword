# Replacement-bank editorial sources

Clues are newly written; the bank does not scrape crossword-publisher clues. References below support factual checks, not a claim of independently fact-checking every record. The TSVs preserve the actual wording and alternatives for review. Editorial tiers describe recall demand provisionally. First names, stage names, abbreviations, removed punctuation and multiword answers are signalled in the clue where needed.

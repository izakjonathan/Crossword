Word sources

New puzzles now use 2,010 answers: 1,910 original entries plus 100 common additions with rewritten crossword clues. The other 3,720 raw dictionary entries are quarantined from new games. The archived data below remains for older saved games. Automated checks do not certify that every clue is unambiguous or enjoyable.

The original 1,910 answers retain their authored clues. The 3,820 supplemental answers use definitions adapted from Princeton WordNet 3.0, Copyright 2006 Princeton University. These direct definitions have automated editorial checks and provisional vocabulary ratings; they have not all been individually test-solved.
